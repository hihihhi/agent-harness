# Policies (SYNTHETIC)

## Quota increases

A quota above the default 200 GB needs approval from the platform owner. Open a ticket with
the size you need and why. Approval takes 3 business days.

## Support hours

The support desk answers on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00. Outside those hours only incidents
that stop every job on the cluster are handled.

## Restoring deleted data

A deleted dataset can be restored from the nightly snapshot for 7 days after it was deleted.
Scratch files are not snapshotted.
